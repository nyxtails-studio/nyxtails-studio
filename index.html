The dark is not empty.

— Nyxtails studio note / 001

We are Nyxtails Studio, an independent team from Türkiye making games for the space between fear and wonder.

Our name is a meeting of opposites: Nyx, the ancient night, and the fox — clever, restless, impossible to fully catch. It is a reminder that atmosphere can be a character, that a quiet hallway can say more than a thousand words, and that the best stories follow you home.

We make worlds with texture. Stories with teeth. Experiences that respect your time, then stay in your head long after you have put the controller down.
